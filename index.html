Simulation assumptions

Virtual sensors use road-user positions. The night test uses a repeating schedule of walkers, cyclists, motorcyclists, cars, and runners. The model assumes 40 W per lamp at full output, a 4 W driver floor plus 36 W proportional to the dimming command, and 2 W for shared adaptive sensors and control. The comparison counts elapsed simulation seconds during the scheduled lighting window. Changes to prototype settings reset the comparison.

These are demonstration estimates. Detection reliability, response time, road illuminance in lux, and actual energy use require physical testing.
